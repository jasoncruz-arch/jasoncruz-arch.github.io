Wahat  Al Musafer (trans. The Traveler's Oasis) is a bus shelter series that embodies innovation and cultural significance. Drawing inspiration from the Abu Dhabi Central Bus Station, completed in 1989 by Bulgarian architect Georgi Kolarov, the design deconstructs the main elements of the whimsical brutalist structure to create a modular system that builds upon the station's legacy.
